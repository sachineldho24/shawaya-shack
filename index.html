Our journey to The perfect roast

Shawaya Shack began with a simple obsession: the vertical rotisserie. By blending the patient techniques of Middle Eastern grilling with the bold, smoky heat of traditional masala, we created a flavor profile that is uniquely ours.

Quality is non-negotiable. From premium-grade poultry to custom-ground masala blends, every element is chosen to honor the fire. This is not fast food; it is fire food.
